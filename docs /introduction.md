# Introduction

## Overview

Know Nepal is a digital platform dedicated to providing structured, reliable, and accessible information about Nepal.

The platform brings together information from multiple domains—including geography, history, culture, tourism, education, healthcare, and wildlife—through a unified web application and REST API.

Our goal is to make Nepal's information easier to discover, understand, and integrate into applications, research, and educational resources.

---

## Mission

To build the most comprehensive digital knowledge platform for Nepal by making accurate information accessible through modern technology.

---

## Vision

To become the trusted platform for discovering, learning, and building applications around Nepal's knowledge and data.

---

## What Know Nepal Provides

- Geographic information
- Administrative divisions
- Historical information
- Cultural heritage
- Tourist destinations
- Educational resources
- Wildlife information
- Healthcare information
- REST APIs for developers

---

## Who Is It For?

Know Nepal is designed for:

- Students
- Researchers
- Developers
- Travelers
- Educational institutions
- Government organizations
- Businesses
- Anyone interested in Nepal

---

## Platform Components

The Know Nepal ecosystem currently includes:

- Web Application
- REST APIs
- Documentation
- Microservices Architecture

---

## Current Status

Know Nepal is actively under development and continues to expand with new datasets, APIs, and features.
